# Getting Started

## Sign up / sign in

Your organization admin creates your account (via invitation) or you
sign up directly, depending on how your deployment is configured. See
[Account Setup](ACCOUNT_SETUP.md).

## Your first document

1. Go to **Documents** in the dashboard sidebar.
2. Upload a file (PDF, Markdown, text, and other common formats are
   supported).
3. Wait for processing — ingestion, chunking, and embedding happen in
   the background; the document's status updates when it's ready to
   search.

See [Documents](DOCUMENTS.md) for supported formats and processing
details.

## Your first question

Once at least one document is ready, go to **Chat**, start a new
conversation, and ask a question. Answers include citations back to the
specific source passages they were grounded in — see
[Citations](CITATIONS.md).

## What's next

- [Agents](AGENTS.md) — configuring a persona/tool-set for repeated use.

[Admin Guide](../admin/ORGANIZATION.md) instead.
