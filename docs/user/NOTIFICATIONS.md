# Notifications

## What triggers a notification

- A workflow step is waiting on your approval.
- You're mentioned or added to a shared conversation.
- An invitation to an organization or team.
- Platform/admin announcements, if your organization sends them.

## Managing preferences

Go to Settings → Notifications to choose which events notify you and
through which channel (in-app, email, and others depending on your
organization's configured integrations — see
[Integrations](../developer/INTEGRATIONS.md)).

## Notification channels for organizations

Admins can route notifications to Slack, Teams, or Discord instead of
(or in addition to) email — see
[`docs/integrations/SLACK.md`](../integrations/SLACK.md),
[`docs/integrations/TEAMS.md`](../integrations/TEAMS.md),
[`docs/integrations/DISCORD.md`](../integrations/DISCORD.md).
