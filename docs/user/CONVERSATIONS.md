# Conversations

## History

All your past conversations are listed in the Chat sidebar, most recent
first. Click one to resume it — new messages have the full prior
context available.

## Sharing

Open a conversation and use **Share** to generate a link. Depending on
your organization's sharing policy, shared conversations may be
viewable by anyone with the link, only organization members, or
disabled entirely — check with your admin if the option isn't
available.

## Deleting

Deleting a conversation removes it and its messages. This cannot be
undone.

## Organizing

Conversations can be associated with a workspace, if your organization
uses workspaces to separate projects or teams.
