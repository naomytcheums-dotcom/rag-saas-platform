# Members & Invitations

## Inviting a member

**Admin → Members → Invite**. Enter an email and assign an initial
role (see [RBAC](RBAC.md)). An invitation email is sent; the invite
expires after a configured period if not accepted.

## Managing existing members

View, change the role of, or remove a member from **Admin → Members**.
Removing a member revokes their access immediately but does not delete
content they created.

## Pending invitations

Pending invitations can be resent or revoked before they're accepted.

## Enterprise SSO

If your organization uses SSO, members may be provisioned automatically
on first login rather than through manual invitation — see [SSO](SSO.md).
