# SaaS

Full detail on the SaaS sales/deployment model is in
[`docs/sales/SAAS.md`](../sales/SAAS.md). This page is the install-guide
pointer.

## What you get

A managed instance — no infrastructure to run, updates and backups
handled for you, billing through the platform's own subscription system
(see [Billing](../admin/BILLING.md)).

## Getting started

Sign up, create your organization, invite members — see
[Getting Started](../user/GETTING_STARTED.md) and
[Organization](../admin/ORGANIZATION.md).

## When to self-host instead

Data residency requirements, deep customization needs, or wanting to
run the platform under your own infrastructure/compliance boundary —
see [Self-hosted](SELF_HOSTED.md) and
[`docs/sales/SELF_HOSTED.md`](../sales/SELF_HOSTED.md).

## White-label / partner

Reselling the platform under your own brand is a separate model — see
[`docs/sales/WHITE_LABEL.md`](../sales/WHITE_LABEL.md) and
[`docs/partners/ONBOARDING.md`](../partners/ONBOARDING.md).
