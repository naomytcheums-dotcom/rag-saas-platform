# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in this project — contributors, maintainers, and anyone
interacting in issues, pull requests, or other project spaces — are
expected to communicate respectfully and in good faith.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or
  political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at
naomytcheums@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed and addressed as
appropriate to the circumstances, up to and including removal of
comments, rejection of contributions, or a temporary or permanent ban
from project spaces.
